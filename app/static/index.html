1 · Trading Mode

Demo mode: the AI trades your demo wallet with real prices, real gas costs, all constraints — simulated settlement.

⚠️ REAL MONEY MODE: the AI will trade the imported wallet's actual funds on DEXs. Your constraints (max loss, position limits, allowed chains) still apply and are enforced in code, but real funds are at risk. DEX swaps are irreversible.

1b · AI Provider Key

The app needs a Mistral API key to make decisions. Paste it here — it is stored encrypted on your machine, never displayed again. (Alternative: put MISTRAL_API_KEY in the .env file.) Get a key at console.mistral.ai.

2 · Constraints for the AI

Hard limits enforced in code on every action — the AI cannot break these.

⚠️ Full control removes ALL constraints except technical limits. Only technical limits apply. Are you sure?

3 · Wallet (real money mode)

Import your hot wallet once. The key is stored encrypted and used only for signing. One EVM key works on all EVM chains (Ethereum, Base, Polygon, BSC, Arc) — same address everywhere. Solana needs its own key (different cryptography).

Type your seed phrase — words are boxed as you type.
Note: seed phrases can't be used for Solana (non-standard derivation) — export its raw key from your wallet.

3b · Auto-discovery of your tokens

Automatically find every token your linked wallet holds. Solana: fully automatic (tokens are enumerable on-chain — nothing to configure). EVM chains (Ethereum, Base, Polygon, BSC, Arc): paste a free Etherscan API key (one key covers all Etherscan-family chains via their V2 API) and the app lists every contract your wallet ever transacted, then reads the real balances on-chain. Without a key, only Watchlist tokens are shown.

3c · Demo Wallet — test with realistic fake funds

Build a fake wallet the bot trades like a real one: real DexScreener prices, real gas costs deducted per transaction, gas reserve enforced — only the blockchain transaction is simulated. Add gas tokens (ETH on base, USDC on arc, ...) and any tokens with amounts you choose, then switch Trading Mode to Real money \u2192 Demo.

4 · Watchlist — extra assets for the AI

Beyond the built-in meme coins, the AI will analyze and price any asset you add here. Data comes from DexScreener (all DEX pairs incl. the Arc ecosystem). Enter token contract addresses (e.g. 0x171a4217b86a807a64eb94757db6849fb4bdbaa0 = cirBTC on Arc) or names/symbols. Comma-separated, up to 30.

Tokens shown = native coins + tokens from your Watchlist. Hold a token that's missing? Add its contract address in Settings \u00b7 4 Watchlist and it appears here (and gets priced for the AI). With an Etherscan key (Settings \u00b7 3b), discovery is fully automatic. Solana tokens are always discovered automatically. TON tokens are not supported.
